Teardown highlights (2026 phone): clip-off back cover (no screws to open) · PH0 Phillips
throughout (~20: 2 battery-bracket long + 6 battery + 10 internal + 1 small + 1 SUB board) ·
main board + SUB board two-board layout (same split as Fairphone 6) · cameras can stick to
mid-frame · no chip-level identifications in the guide.

ODM status: Jolla C2 (2024) was built with **Reeder** (Turkey), Jolla's named manufacturing
partner; the 2026 phone's board-level ODM is not officially disclosed — only Salo final
assembly is public. No FCC filing exists (EU/UK/NO/CH sales only), so no FCC-style internal
photos are available.
